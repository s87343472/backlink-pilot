# Disclaimer & advice (required)

This page states our risk stance on search and links, plus default advice. **Not legal advice. Not a ranking promise.**

## What you must understand

- Search engines (including Google) have long discouraged **link schemes meant to manipulate rankings**. Short bursts of large-scale, low-relevance, or patterned backlinks can be treated as spam or trigger manual / algorithmic actions.
- Links are evaluated by source and quality. Directory links vary widely. **Volume ≠ rankings.**
- This project is a **workflow playbook** for paced Agent work, email handling, and ledgers. It is **not** a black-hat guide and does not guarantee traffic or rankings.

Consult official materials such as [Google Search documentation](https://developers.google.com/search/docs). Policies change; prefer the official text. This page does not quote policies line-by-line or teach “how to evade” enforcement.

## Default advice

1. **Pace:** Keep real submissions to a small daily count (e.g. about 2–4). Do not treat “tens or hundreds of registrations/submits in a day” as recommended.
2. **Relevant & honest:** Accurate product copy; matching categories; location and contact email consistent with your Terms / Privacy Policy.
3. **Diverse & natural:** Directories are one channel. Prefer the product itself, content, and genuine mentions; follow target-repo norms for resource lists.
4. **Revocable:** Keep reciprocal links and badges on `/links` (or an agreed page) so you can remove one entry later.
5. **Paid & forced swaps:** Skip paid boosts and required reciprocals by default. If you raise the tier yourself, you accept the risk.
6. **Monitor:** If Search Console (or similar) looks abnormal, **pause** bulk actions and review. This doc does not cover evasion techniques.
7. **Liability:** You are responsible for site behavior and outcomes from using this playbook; authors/maintainers accept no liability for loss.
